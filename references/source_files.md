# Construction Sources (Not Bundled)

The original source files used while constructing and stress-testing the skill are not bundled in this repository. Case summaries are navigation aids only.

When a delicate theorem condition matters, retrieve or inspect the original paper/book (or a user-supplied copy) before relying on the summary. If the source cannot be accessed, downgrade the claim rather than reconstructing missing conditions from memory.

## Source-use rule

Case summaries in `cases/` are navigation aids. They do not replace the paper's full assumptions, theorem statement, proof, appendix, or notation.
