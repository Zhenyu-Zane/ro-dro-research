# Case: Robust Satisficing

**Source:** Long, Sim, Zhou, *Robust Satisficing*, Operations Research.

## Research pattern

Illustrates how to introduce a new target-oriented decision criterion rather than merely a new ambiguity set. A fragility measure controls how target violation grows with distributional deviation.

## Researcher lessons

- A new robustness paradigm needs a behavioral/decision interpretation, not just a formula.
- Axiomatic characterization can distinguish a new criterion from reparameterized risk measures.
- Tractable specializations should go beyond toy models for a methods contribution.
- Experiments should compare target–fragility frontiers, not only one tuned point.

## Auditor lessons

- Check whether a particular target/radius pair makes the model locally equivalent to a DRO model; local equivalence does not make the paradigms globally identical.
- Verify the probability-distance domain and absolute-continuity restrictions.
- Check finiteness under unbounded support/loss.
- Separate axiomatic representation from computational reformulation.

## Stress test

Determine whether a new target-oriented model is a genuinely new criterion, a scalarization of an existing frontier, or a reparameterization; prove any equivalence only under explicit conditions.
