# Case: Decision-Dependent Information Discovery

**Source:** Vayanos, Georghiou, Yu, *Robust Optimization with Decision-Dependent Information Discovery*, Management Science.

## Research pattern

Endogenous information changes quantifier order and nonanticipativity. Decisions control which uncertain components become observable before recourse.

## Researcher lessons

- Write the information timeline before the robust program.
- A functional decision-rule formulation may have a dynamic min–max–min–max counterpart, but equivalence requires constructive policy mapping.
- K-adaptability can be both approximation and usability constraint.

## Auditor lessons

- Recourse may depend only on revealed uncertainty.
- Check policy construction, tie-breaking, and nonanticipativity.
- Check attainment/nonclosed regions.
- Verify when K-adaptability is conservative and when finite $K$ is exact.
- Distinguish objective uncertainty from uncertain constraints when reformulation properties differ.

## Stress test

Write the quantifier string and nonanticipativity constraints for a first-stage observation decision and audit any claimed dynamic equivalence.
